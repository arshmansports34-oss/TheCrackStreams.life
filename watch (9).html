Quick Fixes When a Stream Misbehaves

Streams depend on live sources, and live sources occasionally hiccup. The fix is almost always one of these, in order:

  • Press Reload Stream once and give the player a few seconds to re-mount.
  • Try Open Direct Link — some sources simply play better in their own tab.
  • Check your connection; the board itself is featherweight, but video needs bandwidth.
  • Still stuck? Hit Report Broken and the match gets flagged on Discord immediately.
